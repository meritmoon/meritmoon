## 📜 1. Lexicon Philosophy & Non-Negotiable Rules

Traditional tech platforms use manipulative gamification: *"Streaks"*, *"XP"*, *"Badges"*, *"Users"*, *"Levels"*. 

**MeritMoon permanently bans superficial gamification vocabulary.** 
Every word in MeritMoon is rooted in thousands of years of authentic meditation tradition. Language must evoke reverence, clarity, and unwavering practice discipline.

### The Golden Vocabulary Rules
1. **Never use "Streak"** $\rightarrow$ Use **"Merits"** or **"Day Merits"**. Practitioners return because they love the truth of practice, not out of streak anxiety.
2. **Never use "User" in practitioner-facing UI** $\rightarrow$ Use **"Practitioner"** (or **"Yogi" / "တရားယောဂီ"**).
3. **Never use "Reset" for intentional restarts** $\rightarrow$ **"Reset"** is *automatic* (triggered by missing a day). **"Restart"** is *intentional* (user choosing to re-experience from Day 1).
4. **"Dana" is not charity or tipping** $\rightarrow$ **Dana** is a sacred practice of generosity that *powers merits protection*.
5. **"Voices" are written reflections** $\rightarrow$ A **"User Voice"** is a genuine, authentic written reflection left for a specific day of practice.

### B. Progress, Merits & Mastery Architecture

| Sacred Term | Definition | Allowed Contexts | ❌ Forbidden Terms |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Merits** | The fundamental measure of course progress and spiritual cultivation. Replaces arbitrary points or streak counters. | Progress headers, milestone dialogs, journey tab. | *"Points", "XP", "Score", "Streaks"* |
| **Day Merits** | Merits secured when all scheduled sessions/exercises of a single day are completed before midnight cutoff. | Daily completion modal, sit overview, calendar. | *"Daily goal", "Day streak", "Check-in"* |
| **Course Merits** | Total accumulated progress along an enrolled course (e.g., *"Day 14 of 30 Merits"*). | Home hero card, course detail meter, journey. | *"Course completion percentage", "Progress bar"* |
| **Reset** | **Automatic event**: Occurs when a Forest Path practitioner fails to complete a day's sessions before local midnight. Active course merits return to Day 1. (Server remembers max merits reached for Moonlit recovery). | Forest failure modal, reminder warnings. | *"Streak lost", "Game over", "Expired"* |
| **Restart** | **Intentional action**: The practitioner consciously chooses to start a course from scratch to earn again merits and re-experience the path with a beginner's mind. | Course options menu, journey tab actions. | *"Reset course", "Wipe progress", "Re-enroll"* |
| **Recovery / Restored Merits** | When a Forest practitioner joins the Moonlit Path, all earned merits up to their historical maximum are instantly restored. | Upgrade prompt, subscription success modal. | *"Unblock", "Recover save", "Pay-to-win"* |
| **Owned ✓** | The permanent sanctuary status granted when a practitioner completes the final day of a course. Unlocked forever for free re-listening. | Journey tab, course detail badge. | *"Purchased", "Downloaded", "Completed item"* |
| **Earn Again Merits** | Action on an **OWNED ✓** course allowing the practitioner to restart active journey mode to cultivate fresh merits. | Owned course detail card. | *"Reset completed course", "Play again"* |
| **Lifetime Records** | Permanent historical archive of total meditation hours, completed courses, and milestones that are never wiped. | Journey tab, profile stats. | *"Stats sheet", "Analytics", "Scoreboard"* |

## ✍️ 4. Voice, Tone & UI Copywriting Guidelines

### 1. In-App Sittings (Tab 3: Sit)
- **Pre-Session**: *"Take a deep breath. Settle into a quiet posture. Close your eyes when you are ready."*
- **Mid-Session Exit Attempt**: *"Leaving now will release this session's merits. Are you sure you wish to step away from the sanctuary?"*
- **Day Complete**: *"Day 7 Merits secured. Carry the stillness of this practice with you into the world. Day 8 unlocks at midnight."*

### 2. The Forest Path Miss / Reset
- **Never scold or guilt-trip**: *"Yesterday's practice was missed. On the Forest Path, active course merits return to Day 1. The path is always waiting for you to begin anew with a fresh mind."*
- **Restore Prompt**: *"Your maximum reached merits (Day 14) are safely recorded. Taking the Moonlit Path will instantly restore all your earned merits."*

### 3. Dana & Teacher Tributes
- *"Dana is powered as a merit. 25% of your Moonlit Path offering flows directly to [Monastery Name] and [Teacher Name] to preserve these ancient teachings for future generations."*
